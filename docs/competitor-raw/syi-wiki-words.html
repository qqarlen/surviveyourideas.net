Survive Your Ideas Words & Idea Examples

Tsunami, Dinosaur, Nuke and Tacos: the four examples the developer actually names.

3 min readUpdated 6 Nov 2026
Evidence notesFour official examples; not a full catalogView sources ↓

The reliable starting point for a Survive Your Ideas words list is the experience's own description. It names four examples: Tsunami, Dinosaur, Nuke and Tacos. This page records those examples without pretending to have a complete catalog of every working word.

The game's “Type ANY word” tagline is an invitation to its premise, not independent proof that arbitrary text always generates a unique event. Spellings, aliases, effects and unlock rules beyond the documented examples still need direct verification.

They do not prove a formal category system, rarity table or damage ranking. The illustrations below are original concepts, not screenshots showing each event's actual appearance.

Is this a complete words list?

No. The official Collector badge asks players to discover 150 words in their Word Diary, making clear that the four promotional examples are not intended as a complete reference. However, that requirement does not reveal all names, the current total, or what action counts as a discovery.

The Word Diary guide covers the confirmed target. Until the catalog is inspected directly, adding dozens of plausible words would make this page look more complete while making it less trustworthy. We prefer exact observations to guesses dressed up as a checklist.

Why a sensible-sounding idea is not verification

A word can sound perfect for a survival game and still be unsupported, restricted or interpreted differently. The same is true of supposed counters: there is no verified rule here that writing a shelter cancels a nuke or another element removes an existing event.

One search result about making a similar Roblox Studio game suggested starter hazards for a new project. Those suggestions describe the author's proposed game, not Survive Your Ideas. They are excluded. When checking a guide, ask whether its author observed this experience or simply described what such a game might contain.

Keep a useful personal word record

If investigating the catalog, record the exact text shown, what you visibly observed and when you checked it. Keep “I saw this event” separate from “I heard this word works.” Note whether you were using a normal writer turn or a paid meta-word feature, because those are different contexts.

This is a suggested research habit, not an official progression mechanic. Keeping notes outside Roblox is not claimed to earn diary progress. Before publishing a stronger entry, useful evidence would show the current game and enough context to distinguish a word from a command, a cosmetic label or an unrelated experience.

Are there best words or winning combinations?

There is not enough verified information to rank words by power. Deadly Idea establishes a goal of eliminating three players with a single word, but does not identify the best way. The stacking premise does not prove any particular combination is reliable.

Use tips for general learning suggestions and commands for the separate reported meta words. If a tier list promises guaranteed wins, check its current evidence. This wiki does not present a damage ranking, combo recipe or counter that the sources cannot support.

New words and the announced update

Trackers report an upcoming event called 40+ WORDS + Word Mastery!, scheduled around 7 November 2026 at 22:00 Asia/Karachi. The announcement suggests more words are planned, but does not provide a verified list of names here.

Read updates before treating announced additions as live. We have not verified the new words, mastery rules or changes to existing examples. This list should expand when the actual release supplies evidence, not because a title creates room for speculation.
