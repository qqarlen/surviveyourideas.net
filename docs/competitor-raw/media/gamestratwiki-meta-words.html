Survive Your Ideas Meta Words: All Unlockable Effects

Meta Words represent intriguing unlockables in Survive Your Ideas that can greatly influence gameplay. Their strategic use can either undermine opponents or enhance your survival odds, adding an extra layer of excitement during matches. To get started with these powerful words, head to the lobby and check the available options in the “Meta Words” menu, where you can trade Cash or Robux for desired effects.

This article delves into Meta Words found in Survive Your Ideas, detailing how to unlock these fascinating items and their gameplay effects.

Meta Words Overview

Players can unlock Meta Words using either Cash or Robux in Survive Your Ideas. They can be employed during matches by typing out the words, following a different format compared to regular inputs. To enact their special effects, a slash (/) prefix is needed; without it, standard game effects will apply instead.

There are six Meta Words obtainable through Cash, and four that require Robux. Below is a comprehensive list of Cash Meta Words along with their effects.

  • /double: Ever wanted to double up your word’s power? This Meta Word lets your next word activate two times instead of one, really cranking up your gameplay! It’s available for a cool 250,000 Cash.
  • /secret: Want to keep your strategies under wraps? This Meta Word makes the words you enter invisible to everyone else on the field. Perfect for sneaky plays, and you can grab it for 250,000 Cash.
  • /lag: Ever dreamed of messing with your friends a little? This Meta Word creates a lag effect for other players, leaving them in the dust while you sail on smoothly. You’ll be safe from this hiccup yourself, and it will cost you 400,000 Cash.
  • /undo: Made a mistake with your last word? No worries! This Meta Word allows you to undo what you just did, giving you a second chance. You can snag it for 400,000 Cash.
  • /ghost: Get ready for some spooky fun! This Meta Word summons ghosts that swoop in to snatch things up. If you’re looking for a fun twist, it’s yours for 500,000 Cash.
  • /jackpot: Want a windfall of Cash? This Meta Word makes it rain money all over the map, giving everyone a reason to celebrate! It’s a money-maker available for 750,000 Cash.

To collect all Cash Meta Words, you’ll need a total of 2,550,000 Cash. You can earn Cash by playing well in matches and completing Index entries.

Next, here’s a rundown of premium Meta Words that are accessible via Robux.

  • /admin: Provides access to admin commands for the duration of the match. Costs 360 Robux.
  • /reset: Resets the game world and heals all players. Costs 199 Robux.
  • /chaos: Gives you access to 10 random words simultaneously. Costs 159 Robux.
  • /god: Ensures your words activate immediately for 30 seconds. Costs 79 Robux.

You can also purchase a bundle of Robux-exclusive Meta Words for 797 Robux, which may drop to 570 Robux during promotions.

Frequently Asked Questions about Meta Words

How many Meta Words does Survive Your Ideas feature?

The game includes six Meta Words that can be unlocked with Cash and four that require Robux, making a total of 10 Meta Words.

How do I obtain the /jackpot Meta Word?

You can get the /jackpot Meta Word for 750,000 Cash.

What is the effect of the /ghost Meta Word?

The /ghost Meta Word summons ghosts that interact with objects in the game.
