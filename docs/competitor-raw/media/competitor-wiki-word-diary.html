Survive Your Ideas Word Diary Guide

A verified collection target, without a guessed catalog or invented rewards.

3 min readUpdated 6 Nov 2026
Evidence notesOfficial Collector badge descriptionView sources ↓

Word Diary is confirmed by the official Collector badge, whose description says: “Discover 150 words in your Word Diary.” That gives players a precise badge target without requiring a guessed full word catalog.

This guide explains what that requirement means, what it does not establish and how to keep collection information accurate. The diary interface and discovery triggers have not been inspected in-game during this research check.

The Collector requirement

Collector asks for 150 discovered words in your Word Diary. The requirement is about discoveries, not winning 150 matches or surviving 150 words in one match. Those are different activities. The official badge was enabled in the checked response.

A target of 150 discoveries is not evidence that the game contains exactly 150 words. It does not tell us whether all entries are available immediately, whether seeing another player's event counts, or whether writing the word yourself is necessary. Those details need verification in the current game rather than inference from a short description.

What can be said about progress?

This wiki can identify the named system and its linked achievement. It cannot supply an accurate progress-screen walkthrough, a category list, entry rarity colors or a reward schedule. No claim button or daily notebook reward has been verified through an official source here.

If checking your own progress, use the labels actually visible in the experience. Compare the screen with the badge description instead of assuming familiar Roblox terminology applies. This matters for a new game where interface details can change quickly.

Organize observations without inventing a checklist

Keep a personal list of exact words you see, their visible effects and whether the diary appears to change afterward. If you cannot tell whether an action counted, label it uncertain. This makes the notes more useful than a list mixing confirmed discoveries with suggestions.

That is an editorial learning method, not an official shortcut or a promise that external notes affect progression. The words page includes only the four developer-named examples. It is not a complete checklist for Collector. A reliable checklist needs actual catalog observations first.

Word Diary is not yet a Word Mastery guide

An upcoming event is titled 40+ WORDS + Word Mastery!, reported for around 7 November 2026 at 22:00 Asia/Karachi. The title introduces a planned mastery feature but does not define its relationship to the diary.

We have not verified whether discoveries feed mastery, whether words gain levels, or whether any bonuses exist. Treating those possibilities as facts would blur an existing badge requirement with an unverified upcoming system. The updates page keeps the announcement and its unknowns separate.

Collection versus other badge goals

My First Idea requires writing your first word in a match. Veteran requires playing 100 matches. Survivor requires surviving 60 words in a single match. None uses the same 150-discovery condition as Collector.

The complete badge guide provides the exact requirements side by side. Choose a goal based on its wording rather than assuming every match activity contributes equally. We have not verified a paid feature that automatically finishes Collector, and this page does not recommend tools claiming to fill a diary for you.
