Survive Your Ideas Script provides a collection of useful utilities for copying words, collecting rewards, tracking players, and viewing game information.

You can copy a selected word, all words from a specific category, or the entire collection using customizable settings. Anti Black Hole provides an additional survival-related option, while the notebook reward feature helps collect available rewards in both the game and lobby.

About Survive Your Ideas

What happens when your imagination becomes reality? In Survive Your Ideas, absolutely anything can happen! Every round, a random player gets to write a word, and that word suddenly comes to life. Your job is to survive whatever appears.
