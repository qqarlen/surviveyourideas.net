How to redeem codes in Survive Your Ideas

Claiming your freebies in Survive Your Ideas is definitely easier than spelling “Apocalypse” under pressure. Here’s what you need to do:

  1. Start Survive Your Ideas in Roblox.
  2. Click on Codes at the top of the screen.
  3. Enter your code in the Type a Code field.
  4. Hit the Redeem button.

Official Wiki, Trello board, and Discord server information for Survive Your Ideas

To stay ahead of the latest disasters and find new words to terrorize your friends with, the official Survive Your Ideas Discord is the place to be.
